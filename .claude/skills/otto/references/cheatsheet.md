# 📖 Otto: the basics

### 🗂️ Plan your table of contents
| Type | You get |
|---|---|
| `/otto outline` | first time: two possible structures from your abstract · later: your outline as an editable board |
| `use A` (or `use B`) | saves that structure as your outline |
| edit on the board, then **Copy changes** and paste | your edits, shown for checking, saved on **go** |
| `/otto outline: [change]` | or just say it: `merge 2.1 and 2.2` · `give chapter 3 500 more words` |

### ⚖️ Discuss your question
🟦 Practitioner · 🟥 Skeptic · 🟩 Economist · 🟨 Historian · 🟪 Academic

| Type | You get |
|---|---|
| `/otto discuss [your question]` | your question through five lenses, as coloured cards |
| `map where they fight` | where they contradict each other, and what nobody covered |
| `write the briefing` | a summary for your supervisor and 5 key findings |
| `grade your work` | how confident each finding is, and the weakest claim |

Example: `/otto discuss whether the PRA should regulate LDI funds directly`

### ✍️ Write
| Type | You get |
|---|---|
| `/otto draft [section]: [your points]` | first a plan to check, then (after you say **go**) the draft, opened beside the chat |
| `/otto polish this, keep my voice: [text]` | your own text, cleaned up |

Example: `/otto draft 3.1: banks only see their own exposure; 2022 showed the gap; my view is…`

### 📄 Your own Word document
Keep it in the **My Thesis Drafts** folder. Otto only reads it, never changes it.

| Type | You get |
|---|---|
| `/otto review 2.2 in my thesis` | feedback on what you wrote: argument, missing footnotes, style |
| `/otto sync my outline` | what changed between your document and your outline |

### 📚 Sources
Drag a PDF in and type `add it` · ask `what do my sources say about [topic]?`

---
More options: `/otto help all` · or just say what you need in your own words.
